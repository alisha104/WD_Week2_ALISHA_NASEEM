ABOUT THE PROJECT

What Is a Sustainable Pakistan?

A sustainable Pakistan is a country where people protect natural resources, reduce pollution and keep their communities clean. Everyone can contribute by saving water, reducing waste, planting trees and choosing environmentally friendly habits.
